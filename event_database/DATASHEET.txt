--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Overview
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This database contains oceanic internal solitary wave (ISW) events detected
in Sentinel-1 IW GRD SAR imagery over the northern South China Sea. Events
were produced by a two-stage machine-learning detection pipeline (SAR stripe
segmentation followed by a context-verifier cascade), then quality-flagged
through manual visual interpretation. Each event carries geometry (crest
count, crest length, propagation direction, wavelength), collocated ERA5
10 m wind, a quality tier, and the review outcome.

Files in this directory:

    events_verified.csv     2816 candidate events that went through the
                            verification/review process; keep_verified=True
                            marks the 2315 verified events
    events.csv              same candidate events, geometry and wind only
                            (no review columns)
    inversion_results.csv   KdV/eKdV amplitude and phase-speed inversion
                            results for 181 high-quality events
    pattern_summary.json    regional pattern statistics (west of Hainan vs.
                            northern SCS slope, split at lon 109.5 E)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Data sources
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - SAR imagery: Sentinel-1A/B Interferometric Wide-swath (IW) Ground Range
    Detected (GRD) products, VV+VH polarization, obtained from the
    Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (https://dataspace.copernicus.eu).
    Scene identifiers are preserved in the "scene" column.
  - Wind: ERA5 hourly 10 m wind ( Copernicus Climate Data Store ),
    collocated to each scene's acquisition time and AOI-mean value.
  - Auxiliary modality (used during model development, not stored per
    event): SWOT L2 KaRIn SSH Expert 2.0 (NASA PO.DAAC).
  - Bathymetry for inversion: SRTM15+.
  - Stratification for inversion: literature-based seasonal climatological
    envelope (no in-situ temperature/salinity profiles were used).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Spatial and temporal coverage
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Area of interest: northern South China Sea,
    approximately lon 106-117 E, lat 16.8-22.6 N.
  - Time range: mainly July-October 2023
    (verified events span 2023-07-01 to 2023-10-10 UTC; a small number of
    June 2023 candidates appear in the full candidate table).
  - Sentinel-1 acquisitions over this AOI are around 10:25/10:50 and
    22:5x UTC (descending/ascending passes).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Size and composition
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - 2816 reviewed candidate events in events_verified.csv.
  - 2315 verified events (keep_verified=True):
        high   quality:  389
        medium quality:  530
        low    quality: 1396
  - 501 rejected candidates (keep_verified=False), with drop_reason:
        nearshore     408   too close to the coast / shallow-water artifacts
        blob_invalid   73   blob-like connected component, no wave geometry
        scene_fp       20   scene-level false positive
  - 181 events with KdV/eKdV inversion results (inversion_results.csv),
    all depression polarity, seasons summer (172) and autumn (9).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Labeling and verification process
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The database was built in four stages:

  1. Model proposals: a Swin-UNet segmentation model detects ISW
     bright/dark stripe patterns in georeferenced Sentinel-1 tiles; scene
     probability mosaics are assembled by overlapping-tile inference.
  2. Crest extraction and event clustering: probability maps (>0.5) are
     skeletonized into crest lines; crests are clustered into wave-packet
     events; geometry (direction, wavelength, length) is computed from the
     crest-line fits with full georeferencing.
  3. Verifier cascade: candidate patches are re-scored by a context
     verifier (center crop + wide context) trained to separate true ISW
     signatures from false alarms (rain cells, wind streaks, speckle,
     land/coastal artifacts), with scene-level leakage-safe splits.
  4. Manual interpretation: QA sheets of candidate events were visually
     reviewed by human interpreters; the keep_verified flag and
     drop_reason column record the outcome. Quality tiers reflect crest
     clarity and confidence (high = clear multi-crest packet; low = faint
     or fragmentary signature).

Wind quality control: ERA5 10 m wind is attached to every event; the
wind_flag column marks whether the wind falls inside the 2-10 m/s imaging
window (in_window / low / high). Events outside the window are retained
but flagged, because SAR ISW visibility degrades outside this range.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Field dictionary — events_verified.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    event_id          unique event identifier
                      (<scene-prefix>_E###, per source scene)
    scene             Sentinel-1 scene name (e.g.
                      S1A_IW_GRDH_1SDV_20230604T102558_...)
    time_utc          scene acquisition start time, UTC, ISO 8601
    lon, lat          event centroid, WGS84 degrees
    n_crest           number of extracted crest lines in the packet
    crest_length_km   summed crest length, km
    direction_deg     propagation direction, geographic azimuth in degrees
                      (direction the packet travels toward)
    wavelength_m      median spacing between adjacent crests, m (empty for
                      single-crest events)
    mean_prob         mean segmentation probability over the event region
    wind_ms           collocated ERA5 10 m wind speed, m/s
    wind_flag         in_window (2-10 m/s) / low (<2) / high (>10)
    bbox_lon          "lon_min,lon_max" of the event bounding box
    bbox_lat          "lat_min,lat_max" of the event bounding box
    area_km2          area of the event region, km^2
    quality           high / medium / low (see section 5)
    dist_coast_km     distance from the event centroid to the coastline, km
    keep_verified     True = verified ISW event; False = rejected
    drop_reason       rejection reason when keep_verified=False
                      (nearshore / blob_invalid / scene_fp)

events.csv contains the same events without the review columns
(keep_verified, drop_reason, dist_coast_km).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Field dictionary — inversion_results.csv (selected fields)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    event_id, scene, time_utc, lon, lat, direction_deg, wavelength_m,
    quality           as above
    depth_m           SRTM15+ water depth at the event location, m
    season            seasonal stratification scenario used
    polarity          wave polarity (all depression in this release)
    regime            kdv_ok / short_wave / djl_needed validity regime
    n_scen            number of stratification scenarios combined
    ekdv_valid        number of scenarios with a valid eKdV root
    c0_ms_med         median linear long-wave speed, m/s
    eta0_kdv_m_med / _lo / _hi    KdV amplitude (m): median and range
    c_kdv_ms_med / _lo / _hi      KdV phase speed (m/s): median and range
    eta0_ekdv_m_med / _lo / _hi   eKdV amplitude (m): median and range
    c_ekdv_ms_med     eKdV phase speed (m/s), median

Inversion assumptions (important):
  - wavelength_m is converted to soliton half-width via l = wavelength/kappa
    with kappa = pi; amplitude estimates are sensitive to this convention.
  - Stratification is a climatological envelope sampled as a 3x3
    sensitivity grid; reported lo/hi are ranges across scenarios, i.e.
    sensitivity intervals, not measurement truth.
  - Events flagged djl_needed exceed the KdV/eKdV validity domain; numbers
    are given for reference only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Known limitations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Seasonal coverage bias: the database mainly covers July-October 2023.
    Winter recall is NOT measurable with the current scene census, so
    wintertime occurrence statistics cannot be derived from this release.
  - Low-quality events (1396 of 2315) are faint or fragmentary detections;
    use them with caution, and prefer high/medium tiers for quantitative
    analysis.
  - Detection visibility depends on wind: SAR ISW signatures are only
    imaged within a soft ~2-10 m/s wind window. Raw event counts are
    therefore NOT occurrence rates; see the visibility-correction code
    (code/evaluation/visibility_correction.py) for the correction method.
  - Wavelength is empty for single-crest events.
  - Direction is reported as a geographic azimuth derived from crest-line
    fitting; for near-axial packets the ambiguity is resolved by regional
    propagation priors, which may occasionally be wrong.
  - Inversion amplitudes are model-based sensitivity intervals (KdV/eKdV
    with climatological stratification), not in-situ measurements.
